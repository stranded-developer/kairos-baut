PT Kairos Multi Sejahtera · Redesign

Four design drafts.

Each draft is a full, responsive home page.

Draft 1

Dark forest green with grid lines that stay visible. Panels widen as you scroll, and the product list opens like an accordion on hover.

Draft 2

Built like a technical drawing sheet. A bolt that draws itself, dimension marks, a title block, and products listed as a filterable table.

Draft 3

Dark showroom look. The brushed steel hero shifts with the cursor, and the product row scrolls sideways while the page is held in place.

Draft 4

Bright and sales-focused. Product search in the hero, big category tiles, a three-step ordering flow, and a green stats band.
